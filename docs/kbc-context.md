# KBC context: what already exists and what the jury will probe

Domain knowledge for every agent and teammate. Verify any number before it goes into the pitch
(use the `kbc-researcher` agent).

## The most important insight

KBC already has a proactive digital assistant: **Kate**, in KBC Mobile since November 2020. KBC
describes Kate as an assistant that proactively offers personalised, relevant solutions at the
right time. That is almost word for word the semi-final challenge.

So the jury will ask, in some form: **"How is this different from Kate?"**
Never position an idea as a competitor to Kate. Position it as something Kate, KBC Live and
advisors can use: a new layer of understanding, a new channel, or a new kind of moment Kate cannot
handle today.

## Facts (public sources)

- Kate launched for private customers in KBC Mobile at the end of November 2020. The name stands
  for "KBC's Assistant To Ease your mind" (earlier: "to ease your life").
  Source: https://newsroom.kbc.com/kate-your-personal-digital-assistant/
- Five years after launch (November 2025), KBC said Kate actively serves 5.8 million digital
  customers across Belgium, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Slovakia and Bulgaria, and that generative
  AI integration is the first step towards a more personal assistant.
  Source: https://www.kbc.com/content/dam/kbccom/doc/newsroom/pressreleases/2025/20251124_Vijf%20jaar%20Kate_EN.pdf
- The **Kate Coins** benefits programme is only available to customers who opted in to
  personalised commercial offers in KBC Mobile. KBC already uses opt-in consent for personalisation,
  so a consent-first design matches how KBC works today. (Same source.)
- When Kate can't help, customers are passed to **KBC Live**, KBC's remote contact centre with human
  advisors. Kate is "digital first", not "digital only".
- KBC's strategy since 2020: products, services and processes are designed "digital first", with
  Kate at the centre, and a feedback loop on the success of Kate's suggestions ("feed the machine").
- The challenge brief mentions more than 2,300,000 customers (KBC Belgium).

## Belgian life moments that make good signals

- Self-employed: quarterly VAT payments on the 20th of the month after each quarter.
- Moving house: register the address change at the town hall, plus employer, health insurance fund
  (mutualiteit), energy supplier, internet provider, insurers.
- First job: student discounts end, first personal income tax bill, no savings buffer.
- Energy: variable versus fixed contracts, winter bills.
- Weather: KMI (Royal Meteorological Institute) warnings per municipality.
- Family: birth, childcare costs, child benefit (Groeipakket in Flanders).

## What a KBC jury will probe

| Angle | Their question | What a strong answer shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale | Does this work for 2.3M customers? What does it cost? | Deterministic detection in batch; AI only for wording, only on change, cached |
| Trust and GDPR | Is this creepy? What is the legal basis? | Opt-in per category, "why am I seeing this", easy correction, pause button |
| Automated decisions | Does the AI decide anything about me? | No. It informs and suggests; credit and other decisions stay with humans and existing processes |
| Fit | Where does this live? Who uses it? | KBC Mobile, Kate, push, KBC Live, branch advisors, all fed from one source |
| Business | Why would KBC build this? | Relevance and trust first; sales follow. At least one card that sells nothing |
| Reality | What is real, what is mocked? | Be explicit. Mocked data is fine, hidden mocks are not |
| Security | How do you protect customer data? | Session-scoped access, no IDs in URLs, Aikido before and after |

## Language

The app copy is English (as in the semi-final). The jury is Belgian; if a pitch is live, check with
the organisers whether English or Dutch is expected.
